# Time-of-day phrases for the Scene node.  Each #@time value is both a
# dropdown option and a filter key: scenes carrying a matching #@time
# directive stay eligible.  Phrases come in indoor and outdoor variants
# (#@setting) so the picked scene's setting selects the matching phrase —
# a studio never gets an outdoor-sky line.  Phrases are trailing clauses
# so they read naturally after the location prose.
#@time: dawn
#@setting: outdoor
first pale light breaking over the horizon, cool air and long soft shadows
#@time: dawn
#@setting: indoor
first pale light through the window, cool air and long soft shadows
#@time: morning
#@setting: outdoor
bright morning light, fresh and clear
#@time: morning
#@setting: indoor
bright morning light through the windows, fresh and clear
#@time: noon
#@setting: outdoor
harsh midday sun, high contrast and short shadows
#@time: noon
#@setting: indoor
harsh midday glare, high contrast and short shadows
#@time: afternoon
#@setting: outdoor
warm afternoon sunlight, gentle contrast
#@time: afternoon
#@setting: indoor
warm afternoon light slanting through the blinds, gentle contrast
#@time: golden hour
#@setting: outdoor
golden hour, low warm sun with long amber shadows
#@time: golden hour
#@setting: indoor
golden hour, warm low sun streaming through the window with long amber shadows
#@time: dusk
#@setting: outdoor
dusk, the sky deepening into violet as lights begin to glow
#@time: dusk
#@setting: indoor
dusk, the room settling into soft amber lamplight
#@time: blue hour
#@setting: outdoor
blue hour, cool twilight with luminous sky and glowing city lights
#@time: blue hour
#@setting: indoor
blue hour, cool twilight seeping in through the windows
#@time: night
#@setting: outdoor
night, selective pools of light against the darkness
#@time: night
#@setting: indoor
night, dim lamplight pooling against the darkness
#@time: midnight
#@setting: outdoor
midnight, deep darkness under a clear starlit sky
#@time: midnight
#@setting: indoor
midnight, deep quiet darkness with faint moonlight through the window
